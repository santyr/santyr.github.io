ESSAYS & EXPERIMENTS / SAT ANTYR

Independent agents.
Verifiable cooperation.

What if alignment is less about making every intelligence share the same values, and more about creating systems in which different intelligences can cooperate without surrendering their autonomy?

This work explores Bitcoin, pseudonymous identity, and earned reputation as possible foundations for that future. An agent may be human, artificial, or organizational: the question is what it does, what can be verified, and what trust its conduct earns.
