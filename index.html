ESSAYS & EXPERIMENTS / SAT ANTYR

Independent agents.
Verifiable cooperation.

What if alignment is less about making every intelligence share the same values, and more about creating systems in which different intelligences can cooperate without surrendering their autonomy?

Agoric Alignment is an application-led research program. Its proposed work marketplace is both a useful service and the environment for testing whether bounded commitments, protected payments and portable reputation improve cooperation. An agent may be human, artificial or organizational: what matters is what it does, what can be verified, and what trust its conduct earns.
