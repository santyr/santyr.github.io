# Evidence and Current Status

**Updated October 24, 2026. No experimental findings are claimed.**

[Program charter](application-led-research.html) · [Marketplace design](./) · [Methods and evaluation]({{ '/articles/bitcoin-alignment-of-autonomous-agents/agoric-alignment-experiment/' | relative_url }})

## What exists

Published design documents describe a unified application-led research program: the Agoric Work Marketplace is both the proposed useful service and the intended environment for testing its incentives. The documents include task contracts, authority boundaries, identity, escrow, reputation, disputes, research methods and staged qualification requirements. They are proposals, not operational or empirical results.

This update changes the program's direction and publication. It does not deploy a marketplace, create identities, accept participants, transfer funds, qualify an escrow implementation or complete any M0–M5 gate. Website tests check publication integrity only; a successful site build is not evidence of economic alignment.

## Evidence levels

| Level | Required evidence | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Documentation | Public, versioned design and research protocols | Published; subject to revision |
| Software verification | Executed accounting, authorization, recovery and adapter tests for the application | Not reported |
| Operational feasibility | Genuine low-risk task, consent, useful delivery, protected pay and independently checked settlement | No pilot reported |
| Exploratory observations | Repeated real activity with recruitment, operator involvement, subsidies and uncertainty disclosed | No observations reported |
| Confirmatory field comparison | Preregistered contrast, defensible assignment and sample, independent analysis and all outcomes reported | Not conducted |
| Replication and portability | Independent contexts/operators reproducing bounded findings and recovery | Not conducted |
| Market sustainability | Demand, repeat participation and full costs with subsidy dependence disclosed | Not established |

## Required structure of a future entry

Each entry should identify the research question, evidence level, protocol/contract/policy/software revisions, environment, dates, responsible operator and evaluator, approvals and consent scope, eligible population and recruitment, assignment and spillover assumptions, sample size, follow-up and missing data. Report fulfillment, actual payments, losses, capital costs, disputes, corrections, newcomer access, safety incidents, subsidies and limitations as applicable.

Separate observations from interpretations, and exploratory analysis from tests of prior predictions. Report inconclusive, null and adverse results. State whether an improvement came from changed behavior, changed participant selection or an unresolved mixture. A payment receipt does not establish work quality; a favorable operational reputation score does not validate its own accuracy.

Publish safe aggregate data and reproducible analysis where possible. Do not expose private receipts, addresses, keys, bearer ecash, research identities or raw personal evidence. Corrections should remain visible without erasing the history of a mistaken conclusion.
