## Historical thesis vs evidence

The essay proposes that Bitcoin might have emerged from an Extropian project anticipating a world of autonomous nonhuman economic actors. The technical and philosophical lineage is substantiated in archival writings; the intent and a Finney–Sassaman co-creator theory remain speculative. Research must not use the proposed authorship as a causal premise or evidence of protocol security.

Leary's influence offers the methodological approach: actively construct, vary and test possible futures. The agoric paper supplies the substantive market design; Finney/Sassaman the autonomy and identity/privacy constraints; Szabo the reputational incentive model; Bitcoin a competing choice of externally verifiable economic history. Thus no historical author is treated as having already solved the research question.
