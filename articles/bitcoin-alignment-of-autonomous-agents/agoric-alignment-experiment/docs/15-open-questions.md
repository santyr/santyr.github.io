# 15 — Open decisions for useful work and credible evidence

The program direction is settled: **the marketplace is the application and the primary test environment**. The questions below determine implementation and evaluation within that direction; they are not a reason to restart the old simulator-first project. No live approvals are implied.

## Before M0–M1

1. Which genuine low-risk task and acceptance criteria provide the smallest useful end-to-end workflow? Who has the need, who authorizes it and which participants are accountable?
2. Which exact supported settlement construction protects earned compensation, bounds concurrent exposure and recovers from signer/mint/network failure? Is the selected Cashu condition actually enforced, or is a reviewed alternative necessary?
3. What minimal worker interface and bounded agent tools permit negotiation, refusal, scope confirmation, evidence and appeal without exposing wallet or account authority?
4. Which identity/keymaster capabilities are genuinely necessary for the pilot? What independent verification is required for the claims being made, and which anchoring studies can wait?
5. Where will the shared domain implementation live, how will environments be isolated and how will schemas/adapter contracts be versioned? Repository structure must not recreate two different escrow engines.

## Before M2

Specify the responsible sponsor, legal contracting arrangement, task admissibility, compensation, expenses, escrow/beneficiary rules, maximum financial exposure, consent, data retention, reviewer/arbitrator, incident contact and recovery. Obtain review appropriate to the actual custody, labor, privacy and research arrangement. What must a worker understand before acceptance, and how will genuine refusal or an emergency be handled without unjust sanction?

How will project-funded demand, onboarding incentives and manual intervention be disclosed? The first task should meet a real need; it must not be a wash trade to manufacture reputation. A supervised pilot can establish feasibility while honestly not establishing autonomous or sustainable operation.

## Before M3–M4

What is the first precisely defined policy contrast: collateral, exposure or a declared bundle? Which common protections and other marketplace factors remain comparable? Who can afford participation, who declines and what data can legitimately be collected about those decisions?

What assignment unit and exposure map address repeated participants, shared counterparties, multiple identities and durable reputation? Are there enough independent units for causal inference, or only enough for descriptive learning? How will spillovers, attrition, rare harms and selective auditing affect precision?

What independent evidence can establish useful fulfillment and actual payment without treating the platform rating as truth? How are uncertain outcomes, complaints, final findings and reversals distinguished? Which success, loss, cost and newcomer-access endpoints will be primary, and which harm bounds are nonnegotiable?

## Later mechanisms

Only after basic operation and measurement are credible should the program decide whether pooled collateral, guarantee networks, insurance, advanced evaluators or anchoring comparisons justify their complexity. How will double pledging, guarantor concentration, correlated issuers and arbitrator incentives be constrained? Can existing simple per-job funding produce better outcomes at lower burden?

## Decision records

Record each decision's owner, alternatives, rationale, affected assumptions, testable acceptance criteria, permission dependencies and unresolved uncertainty. Preserve dissenting and negative evidence. A published ADR is a design commitment, not proof of implementation or a grant of operational authority. Use the [gates](11-stage-gates.md) and [backlog](16-implementation-backlog.md) to keep the next work tied to useful application progress.
